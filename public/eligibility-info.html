✅ Eligibility Criteria

Several factors significantly boost your chances of winning. Each criterion earns you points, increasing your probability of being selected:

1. Points Accumulated Since the Start of the Game

The more points you’ve earned, the higher your chances of being selected.

2. Number of Quests Completed

Every completed quest counts. Completing more quests increases your chances of winning.

3. Number of Friends Invited

Inviting more friends earns you more points, which boosts your chances of receiving an airdrop.

✅ Bonuses

Boost your chances of winning one or more airdrops with exclusive bonuses! There are two types of bonuses available:

1. NFTs

You can mint up to 5 NFTs directly from your profile by connecting your wallet.

Each minted NFT enhances your chances of receiving an airdrop. Holding all 5 NFTs grants you an even greater bonus!

2. $JOK Holders

Holding JOK Tokens rewards you with additional bonuses based on your grade. (Make sure to paste your ERC-20 address in your profile if you are on the ETH network.)

Grade JOK Required
1/5 500K JOK
2/5 1M JOK
3/5 5M JOK
4/5 25M JOK
5/5 50M JOK

Each grade level increases your airdrop chances, with an extra bonus for reaching the maximum grade (5/5)!

✅ Random Distributions

All distributions are random and executed by our algorithm. A significant portion of our company’s revenue is redistributed through these airdrops, rewarding our users for their engagement and loyalty.
